How we coach

Our unique methodology.

Our coaching program meets students at their level — whether you're just learning the game or pushing toward an expert rating. We focus on 12 fundamental concept areas that cover the full range of chess skills. We begin by understanding each student's specific needs, then build a fully personalized curriculum using our extensive library of curated puzzles and model games to turn chess knowledge into consistent, winning habits.
