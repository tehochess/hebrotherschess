How we coach

Our unique methodology.

A clear pattern we realized as coaches was that many students plateau because they don't know their weaknesses and make the same mistakes over and over. What makes our coaching program unique is our carefully curated assessment that systematically reveals a student's strengths and weaknesses. Using the assessment results, we build a tailor-made plan for each lesson, utilizing thousands of compiled puzzles and model games over the years that provide a structured curriculum that turns abstract concepts into practical, winning habits.

Services

Our services

👥
Group lessons
Small group sessions covering core concepts, puzzles, and model games. Effective way to build a strong foundation alongside other motivated players.
♟
Private lessons
One-on-one sessions built entirely around your strengths, weaknesses, and goals. Tailored material every session based on your assessment results.
⚔️
Training matches
Play a game against one of us, then go through it together. Annotated PGN provided so you can review on your own. One of the fastest ways to improve.
🏆
Tournament opportunities
We have lots of creative tournament ideas and formats to run ourselves, best suited for students' growth and local community engagement.
