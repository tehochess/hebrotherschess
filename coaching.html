How we coach

Practical chess.
Real improvement.

Most players plateau because they don't know their weaknesses — they keep showing up to tournaments, repeating the same mistakes, and not making progress. Our job is to find those gaps, give you targeted material to address them, and build the kind of practical skill that holds up in real games. We use engines extensively and know how to translate what they show into things a human can actually apply.
