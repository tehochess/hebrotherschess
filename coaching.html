How we coach

Our unique methodology.

A clear pattern we realized as coaches was that many students plateau because they don't know their weaknesses and make the same mistakes over and over. Our coaching program tackles player plateaus by focusing on 12 fundamental concept areas where students typically struggle. We begin by diagnosing a student's specific needs through a combination of skill assessments and deep analysis of their gameplay. From there, we construct a fully personalized curriculum, leveraging our extensive library of curated puzzles and model games to transform abstract theory into consistent, winning habits.
