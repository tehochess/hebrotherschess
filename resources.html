Resources

A curated list of books, sites, and tools we recommend to our students. These are resources we've personally used and believe in.

📚 Recommended books
♟
How to Reassess Your Chess
Jeremy Silman
A foundational text on imbalances and positional thinking. Teaches you how to identify what's happening in a position and form a plan accordingly.
Intermediate – Advanced
♟
The Amateur's Mind
Jeremy Silman
Silman analyzes games from amateur players and explains the thinking errors behind common mistakes. Very practical for self-improvement.
Beginner – Intermediate
♟
Silman's Complete Endgame Course
Jeremy Silman
Endgames organized by rating level — you only study what's relevant to your current strength. One of the most practical endgame books ever written.
All levels
♟
The Art of Attack in Chess
Vladimir Vukovic
The definitive guide to attacking chess. Covers king safety, piece coordination, sacrifices, and mating patterns with deep explanations and examples.
Intermediate – Advanced
♟
Chess Structures
Mauricio Flores Rios
A thorough breakdown of pawn structures and the plans that arise from them. Essential reading for understanding positional chess at a deep level.
Intermediate – Advanced
