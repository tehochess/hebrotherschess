About us

Structured training for the serious chess improver.

Daniel and Samuel He are identical twins from the Seattle area. They each achieved the National Master title at the start of high school through the local tournament circuit, the same scene many of their students are part of. They coached the Redmond High School chess team to the 2017 State Championship and a top-5 finish at Super Nationals. They hope to share their passion for chess with others, whether nurturing up and coming prodigies or serious adult improvers.
